BDIF is a database that allows you to consult and download AMF decisions and financial disclosures on issuers.

To find out how long the types of information on BDIF are kept, please visit Need help?

The decisions and disclosures are shown in their original format and the AMF does not guarantee the completeness of the decisions prior to 2008.

 

New BDIF - RSS feed subscription is evolving, with the tracking of publications by companies: https://bdif.amf-france.org/en/navigation-flux-rss
